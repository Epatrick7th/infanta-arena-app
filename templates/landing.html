A cockpit built
for the province.

Infanta Sports Arena stands on the road into town, red against the rice fields, with the mountains behind it. It was raised for the sport this province has always kept: the derby, the crowd, the long afternoon that runs into night.

Every fight is watched by everyone at once. That is the whole point of a room like this.

The house

2026

The arena opens

Built in Infanta and finished in 2026. Red walls, a clear span roof, and a pit the whole gallery can see into.

The setting

Fields on every side

The arena sits among working farmland with the range rising behind it. People come in from the barangays on the road that passes the front door.

The mark

Gold, red and the pit

The crest carries the two birds meeting under the lights, ringed in gold, with the year the house was established.
