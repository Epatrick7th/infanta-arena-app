Know before you go

How the fighting is run

01

Derby

Entries entered as a team, usually three, five or seven cocks. The entry taking the most wins across the card takes the derby. This is where the serious breeders test a whole programme, not one bird.

02

Traditional sabong

Single matched fights, made on the day between two owners once the birds are weighed and paired. The backbone of any regular fight day.

03

On the day

  • Arrive early for a seat with a clear line to the pit.
  • Bring cash. Gate and betting are cash.
  • Bets are called and settled through the kristo.
  • Handlers and referee are not to be interfered with.

Legal notice. Infanta Sports Arena is a licensed physical cockpit. Traditional cockfighting is regulated in the Philippines under Presidential Decree 449 and is permitted on days allowed by law and local ordinance. Online cockfighting (e-sabong) is suspended under Executive Order No. 9 (2022); this arena does not operate, offer or accept online betting. All betting takes place in person at the licensed venue.

Gamble responsibly. Betting can be addictive. Wager only what you can afford to lose. Strictly for those aged 21 and over. If gambling is causing harm to you or someone you know, contact the NCMH Crisis Hotline on 1553 or 0917-899-8727.
